# Ticketly Hub – Event Ticket Scanner & Check-In App for Event Tickets Plus and The Events Calendar

**Ticketly Hub** is a fast, modern **Android QR ticket scanner and attendee check-in app** for WordPress event websites running **Event Tickets Plus** and **The Events Calendar**.

Scan tickets at the door, check attendees in and out, work offline, bulk check-in groups, send reminder emails, and export PDF attendee reports, all from your phone and all connected directly to your own WordPress site.

> ⭐ **If Ticketly Hub makes your event check-ins easier, please [star this repository](#-support-the-project) and [rate us on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.appcluster.events_ticket_scanner).** It helps other event organizers find the app.

> **Independent Project**
> Ticketly Hub is an independent, open-source app. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by** the creators of Event Tickets Plus or The Events Calendar. It is built to be **compatible** with those plugins so organizers get a simple, feature-rich alternative for mobile check-in.

## 🚀 Why Ticketly Hub?

Long queues at the entrance are the fastest way to frustrate attendees. Ticketly Hub is built for event staff who need check-in to **just work**, even when the venue Wi-Fi doesn't.

- ⚡ **Scan and check in within seconds** – point, scan, done
- 📴 **Keep working offline** – no connection, no problem
- 👥 **Handle crowds** – bulk check-in for groups and peak entry times
- 📧 **Bring no-shows back** – send reminder emails to attendees who haven't arrived
- 📄 **Report instantly** – generate PDF attendee reports from your phone
- 🌍 **Staff-friendly** – available in English, German, Spanish and French
- 🔒 **Private by design** – talks only to *your* WordPress site; no Ticketly servers in between

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎫 Fast QR Ticket Scanning
Scan attendee QR code tickets instantly with your device camera for quick, accurate event entry.

### ✅ Real-Time Check-In & Check-Out
Attendee status syncs with your WordPress website in real time, so your Event Tickets Plus attendee list always stays up to date.

### 📦 Bulk Check-Ins
Check in multiple attendees at once to cut waiting times during peak entry.

### 📧 Automatic Email Reminders
Send reminder emails to attendees who haven't checked in yet.

### 👥 Smart Attendee Management
- Search attendees instantly
- Filter by ticket type
- View attendee details
- Organize large attendee lists with ease

### 📴 Offline Mode
Previously synced events and attendees stay available on the device, so staff can keep checking guests in without an internet connection.

### ⭐ Favorite Events
Pin the events you use most for one-tap access.

### 📄 PDF Report Generation
Create attendee reports directly on your phone for sharing or record keeping.

### 🌍 Multi-Language Support
English · German · Spanish · French — more languages planned.

### 🎨 Modern Material Design
A clean, lightweight interface designed for fast use at a busy entrance.

## 🛠 How It Works

1. **Install** Ticketly Hub from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.appcluster.events_ticket_scanner).
2. **Connect** the app to your WordPress website running Event Tickets Plus and The Events Calendar.
3. **Pick an event** and your attendee list syncs to the device.
4. **Scan tickets** at the door — check-ins sync back to your site in real time (or later, if you're offline).
5. **Export a PDF report** when the event is over.

---

## 🔌 Compatibility

Ticketly Hub works with WordPress event websites using:

- **Event Tickets Plus**
- **The Events Calendar**

The app communicates directly with your own WordPress website to fetch events, sync attendee information and manage check-ins. **No Ticketly-operated servers are involved**, so your attendee data stays between your phone and your site.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is Ticketly Hub the official Event Tickets Plus app?</b></summary>

No. Ticketly Hub is an independent, open-source app built to be compatible with Event Tickets Plus and The Events Calendar. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by their creators.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I use Ticketly Hub to scan Event Tickets Plus QR codes?</b></summary>

Yes. Ticketly Hub scans attendee QR code tickets from WordPress sites running Event Tickets Plus and checks attendees in on your site.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it work without internet?</b></summary>

Yes. Events and attendees you've already synced stay available offline, so your staff can continue checking guests in.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where is my attendee data stored?</b></summary>

On your own WordPress website and your device. Ticketly Hub connects directly to your site — there are no Ticketly servers in between.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can multiple staff members scan at the same event?</b></summary>

Check-ins sync with your WordPress site in real time, so every connected device works from the same up-to-date attendee status.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is there an iOS version?</b></summary>

The iOS version is coming soon. ⭐ Star or watch this repository to be notified when it launches.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is Ticketly Hub free and open source?</b></summary>
