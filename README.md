# Ticketly – Event Ticket Scanner for WordPress

A modern Android app for **QR ticket scanning**, **attendee check-ins**, and **event management** on compatible WordPress event websites.

Ticketly is an independent, open-source mobile companion app built for event organizers who use **Event Tickets Plus** together with **The Events Calendar**. It provides a fast, reliable, and user-friendly experience for managing attendees directly from your Android device.

> **Independent Project**  
> Ticketly is independently developed and is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by** the creators of Event Tickets Plus or The Events Calendar.

---

# Why Ticketly?

Managing attendees at busy events should be simple.

Ticketly helps event organizers reduce queues, speed up attendee check-ins, and manage events efficiently with a clean, modern interface designed specifically for event staff.

Whether you're organizing a conference, workshop, concert, festival, meetup, school event, corporate gathering, or community event, Ticketly keeps your check-in process fast and reliable.

# Key Features

### 🎫 Fast QR Ticket Scanning

Scan attendee QR codes instantly using your device's camera for quick and accurate event entry.

### ✅ Real-Time Check-In & Check-Out
Synchronize attendee status with your WordPress website in real time so your event dashboard always stays up to date.

### 📦 Bulk Check-Ins
Process multiple attendees quickly to reduce waiting times during peak entry periods.

### 📧 Automatic Email Reminders
Send reminder emails to attendees who have not yet checked in.

### 👥 Smart Attendee Management
- Search attendees instantly
- Filter by ticket type
- View attendee information
- Organize large attendee lists

### 📴 Offline Mode
No internet?
No problem.
Previously synchronized events and attendees remain available, allowing staff to continue checking in guests even without an internet connection.

### ⭐ Favorite Events
Pin frequently used events for one-tap access.

### 📄 PDF Report Generation
Generate attendee reports directly from your phone for sharing or record keeping.

### 🌍 Multi-Language Support
Available in:

- English
- German
- Spanish
- French

More languages are planned for future releases.

# Compatibility

Ticketly is designed for compatible WordPress event websites using:

- **Event Tickets Plus**
- **The Events Calendar**

The application communicates directly with your own WordPress website to retrieve events, synchronize attendee information, and manage check-ins.

No Ticketly-operated servers are involved.
